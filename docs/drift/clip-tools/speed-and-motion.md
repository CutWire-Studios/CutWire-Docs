# Speed and motion

Change how fast a clip plays, reverse it, fade edges, or draw a custom speed
curve.

![Speed & Fade properties](../assets/speed-fade.avif)

## Constant speed

Select a video or audio clip → **Properties → Speed & Fade**:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Playback speed** | Rate slider / field. |
| Presets | Quick picks from **0.25×** to **4×**. |
| **Reverse** | Play the clip backwards. |

## Fades

Fade in and fade out controls soften the start and end. **Fade style** options:

| Style | Feel |
|---|---|
| **Linear** | Constant ramp. |
| **Smooth** | Eased curve. |
| **Natural** | Gentler perceptual fade. |

## Custom speed curve

1. Click **Custom speed…** to open the **Custom speed** window.
2. Add points on the curve (**Add point**).
3. Choose **Sharp** or **Smooth** per point; delete or **Reset** as needed.
4. Preview, then **Apply** (Drift can place a copy on a new track) or **Cancel**.

When a custom curve is active, use **Custom speed active — remove** to clear it
and return to constant speed.

## Beat-oriented looks

For motion synced to music, try beat-oriented [templates](./templates) such as
Beat Drop, Slowmo Drop, or Storm Drop with your soundtrack on the timeline.
