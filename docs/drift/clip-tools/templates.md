# Templates

**Templates** are ready-made look packages — often timed to music — that stack
effects and motion for you.

![Templates assets tab](../assets/templates.avif)

## Apply a template

1. Open **Assets → Templates**.
2. Browse by category chips (Hype, Dreamy, Cinematic, Clone, Anime, Chaos, Drama,
   Transition, and others).
3. Drag a template onto a clip (or follow on-screen placement hints).

Some templates show **Needs cutout** — run **Cut out subject…** first (see
[Cutouts and masks](./cutouts-and-masks)). Beat-oriented templates work best with
music on an audio track.

like any other edit.
