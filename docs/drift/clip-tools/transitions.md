# Transitions

Transitions blend from one clip into the next where they overlap on the same
track.

![Transitions panel](../assets/transitions-browser.avif)

Drag a transition onto where two clips overlap:

![Overlapping clips with a transition](../assets/transitions.avif)

## Place a transition

1. Overlap two clips on a video track (or leave a join you can bridge).
2. Open **Assets → Transitions**.
3. Drag a transition onto the overlap — the UI tip: *Drag onto where two clips
   overlap…*

You can also select the join and use **Properties → Transition** →
**Add crossfade (0.5 s)** for a quick default.

## Transition inspector

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Which transition style to use. |
| **Duration (s)** | How long the blend lasts. |
| Parameters | Style-specific knobs when available. |
| **Remove transition** | Clear the transition from the join. |

## Bundled transitions

| Category | Transitions |
|---|---|
| **Basic** | Crossfade, Dip to Black, Dip to White, Push Left, Wipe Down, Wipe Left, Wipe Right, Wipe Up, Zoom In |

| **Grid & Geometric** | Honeycomb Hexagon, Linear Blinds, Pixelate Matrix, Triangle Mosaic, Voronoi Shatter |
| **Glitch & Digital** | Analog Interference, Digital Matrix Rain, RGB Displacement, VHS Scanline |
| **Particle & Liquid** | Bubbling Dissolve, Ink Bleed, Liquid Smudge, Particle Wind, Plasma Burn |
