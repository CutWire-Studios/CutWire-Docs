# Getting started

This page walks you from a fresh install to your first exported video. It assumes
Drift is already installed and open.

## 1. Choose a video layout

On first launch (or when you start a **new project**), Drift asks you to
**Choose your video layout**. Pick a platform template — YouTube, Instagram,
TikTok, and more — and a quality (**4K**, **1080p**, or **720p**), then click
**Continue**.

Choose **Decide later** to keep the default size; you can change it anytime in
[Settings](./settings).

![Choose your video layout dialog](./assets/layout-chooser.avif)

## 2. Import media

Open the **Media** tab in the assets panel (left), then:

- Click **Import**, or
- Drag video, audio, or image files onto the Media tab.

Supported types include MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, MP3, WAV, PNG, JPG, GIF, and more.
Imported items appear as thumbnails you can drag onto the timeline.

See [Media library](./media-library) for every assets tab.

## 3. Build on the timeline

Drag a clip from **Media** onto a video track. Use the timeline tools to shape it:

| Tool | What it does | Key |
|---|---|---|
| **Select** | Move and select clips | `V` |
| **Cut** | Click a clip to split it | `B` |
| **Trim start / Trim end** | Drop everything left or right of the click | — |

Press **Space** to play or pause. Drag clip edges to trim. Enable **snapping** and
**ripple** from the timeline toolbar when you want edges to stick and gaps to close.

More detail: [Timeline](./timeline).

## 4. Polish the look

Select a clip and use the **Properties** panel on the right:

- **Position & size** — place, scale, rotate, flip.
- **Effects** — browse GPU effects from the **Effects** assets tab, or apply from Properties.
- **Speed & Fade** — change speed, reverse, fade in/out.
- **Text / Stickers / Shapes** — add overlays from their assets tabs.

![Effects browser in the assets panel](./assets/properties-transform.avif)

See [Editing clips](./editing-clips) and [Clip tools](./clip-tools/text-and-titles).

## 5. Export

Click **Export** in the header. Pick a downscale size (or keep the project size),
optionally open **Advanced** for encoder and quality, then choose a save location
and click **Export**.

Drift exports what you see in the preview. Full options: [Export](./export).
