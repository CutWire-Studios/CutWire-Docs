**Create polished videos fast — free, open, and yours.**

Drift is a free, open-source desktop video editor from CutWire Studios. Drop in
clips, add effects and stickers, generate captions, and export — with no
subscription, no watermark, and no account required.

![The Drift editor with assets, preview, properties, and timeline](./assets/main-window.avif)

Preview and export share one compositor, so what you see is what you get.

## What you can do with it

- **Multi-track timeline** — trim, split, snap, ripple, mute/hide tracks, and full undo/redo.
- **Effects & transitions** — GPU effects, stylish transitions, and reusable look templates.
- **Stickers, titles & shapes** — finish the look without leaving the editor.
- **Auto captions** — speech-to-text captions you can edit on the timeline.
- **Cutouts & masks** — isolate subjects, mask clips, and key out green screens.
- **Speed & motion** — speed changes, reverse, fades, and animate to the beat.
- **Audio tools** — mixing, effect chain, and background noise cleanup.
- **Addons** — optional fonts, stickers, speech models, and acceleration runtimes.
- **Project bundles** — package a project with its media for sharing and backup.
- **Export** — choose size and codec so the file matches what you saw in preview.

## How the pieces fit together

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Assets panel** | Left sidebar for Media, Sounds, Text, Stickers, Shapes, Effects, Templates, Transitions, Settings, and Shortcuts. |
| **Preview** | Center viewer for the composition at the playhead — Fit/Fill, quality, guides, fullscreen. |
| **Properties** | Right panel for the selected clip — timing, transform, effects, audio, and more. |
| **Timeline** | Bottom multi-track editor — video, audio, text, subtitle, and shape lanes. |
| **Extras** | Addon Manager in the header — install fonts, stickers, AI models, and runtimes. |
