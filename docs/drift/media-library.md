# Media library

The left **Assets** panel is where you import files and browse built-in content.
Switch tabs along the side; most tabs let you drag items onto the timeline.

![Assets panel Media tab](./assets/assets-media.avif)

## Media

Imported video, image, and audio files.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Import** | Pick files from disk. |
| **Grid / List** | Toggle thumbnail grid or list view. |
| **Sort by name / Sort by type** | Order the library. |

Drag files onto the Media tab to import. While dragging you’ll see *Drop to
import* — *Video, audio and image files*. Large imports show *Importing…*.

Supported extensions include: `.mp4`, `.mov`, `.mkv`, `.avi`, `.webm`, `.m4v`,
`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.aac`, `.flac`, `.ogg`, `.m4a`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`,
`.webp`, `.bmp`.

## Sounds

Audio-effect presets (EQ, voice FX, space, texture, and more). Drag onto an
audio or video clip, or apply from the clip’s **Audio** properties. See
[Audio](./clip-tools/audio).

## Text

Create titles and captions:

- Add a text clip (blank or with typed content).
- **Add subtitle clip** for a subtitle lane clip.
- **Import subtitle file** (`.srt`).
- **Add auto caption** — speech-to-text (needs the Whisper / AI addons).

Details: [Text and titles](./clip-tools/text-and-titles) and
[Captions](./clip-tools/captions).

## Stickers

Emoji and sticker packs by category. If packs aren’t installed yet, Drift points
you to **Extras**. Use **More emoji** for the full picker. See
[Stickers and shapes](./clip-tools/stickers-and-shapes).

## Shapes

Built-in vector shapes in categories:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Basic** | Rectangle, Ellipse, Triangle, Diamond, Hexagon, … |
| **Arrows** | Arrow, Double arrow, Chevron, … |
| **Bubbles** | Speech bubble, Thought bubble, Callout |
| **Fun** | Star, Heart, Burst, Cloud, Lightning bolt, … |

Click or drag onto the timeline; restyle in the **Shape** properties tab.

## Effects

GPU video effects grouped by category (Glitch, Retro, Dreamy, Impact, Funny Face,
Artistic, Keying, and more). Drag onto a visual clip or browse from Properties →
**Effects**. Catalog and workflow: [Effects](./clip-tools/effects).

## Templates

Music-synced look templates (Anime Punch, Beat Drop, Cinematic Open, and more).
Some need a cutout or music on the timeline. See [Templates](./clip-tools/templates).

## Transitions

Transition cards. Tip in the UI: *Drag onto where two clips overlap…* Full list
and inspector: [Transitions](./clip-tools/transitions).

## Settings

Project video size, layout chooser, crop canvas, preview guides/background, and
update checks — documented on [Settings](./settings).

## Shortcuts

Browse and rebind keyboard shortcuts — [Hotkeys](./hotkeys).
