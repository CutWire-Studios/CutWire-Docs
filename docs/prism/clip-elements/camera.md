# Camera

A **Camera** element brings a live webcam or capture device into your show — a USB
webcam, a capture card, or any video input the system recognises.

**Add it:** **Add Element → Camera…**, then choose a device from the list.

## Choosing a device

The picker lists every detected video input by name. If you don't choose a
specific one, pick **Default Camera** and CutWire Prism lets the system decide. The list
combines devices reported by the OS with any additional capture nodes found on the
machine, so a capture card showing your main camera or console feed will appear
here too.

## What it can do

- Stream live to either deck with low latency.
- **Transform and position** on a canvas via the card's inline **Transform**
  controls — drop a presenter cam into a corner over other content.
- Crossfade between two cameras (one on each deck) for a simple multi‑camera cut.

## Tips

- Pair a camera on one deck with a [text](./text) or
  [HTML overlay](./html-overlay) on a canvas to add names and scores over a live
  feed.
- For a phone instead of a webcam, use the [Phone camera](./phone-camera) element,
  which streams over Wi‑Fi with no cable.
